v1.0.0 (10/11/2026)
- KSNV Report is now its own app, separate from Claim & Equipment Tools.
- Fills the KSNV Google Sheet of PVI Digital: CẤP ĐƠN (policies per day), THỰC HIỆN (revenue of the month and year to date), Tái tục (LƯU 2026) and RÀ SOÁT XCG (results of the XCG review).
- THỰC HIỆN takes the revenue of the month and the year-to-date total straight from the revenue report (BAO CAO THUC HIEN, sheet Chi tiet thang năm NV), so the totals match that report.
- RÀ SOÁT XCG: import the review file the business units filled in (Rà soát cấp đơn XCG), and the tool copies their answers into column Q of the tab, matched by policy, certificate number and review item.
- Every write saves the old cells first and can be undone.
